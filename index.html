The journey

Five steps, one person holding the thread

From the moment your flight lands to the follow-up call after you are home, the same coordinator stays with your case.

01

Arrival & pickup

Airport pickup, transfer to your accommodation, SIM and payment setup, and a first briefing on what happens next.

02

Registration & consultation

Hospital registration, department matching and an in-person consultation with the specialist, interpreted line by line.

03

Examination & diagnosis

Imaging, laboratory work and specialist review, sequenced to avoid repeat tests and unnecessary waiting.

04

Treatment & recovery

Surgery or therapy, inpatient stay, nursing coordination and rehabilitation, with daily updates to your family.

05

Discharge & follow-up

Discharge summary in English, medication plan, return transfer, and scheduled follow-up after you are back home.

What we arrange

Everything around the medical care

Clinical decisions belong to the hospital. Everything that surrounds them belongs to us.

  • Airport pickup and all ground transfers
  • Hospital registration and appointment booking
  • English interpretation at every consultation
  • Medical record translation and case summary
  • Accommodation near the hospital
  • Inpatient coordination and family updates
  • Discharge documents and medication plan in English
  • Follow-up scheduling after you return home

How a case starts

Send us the diagnosis, the latest imaging and lab reports, and what you are hoping to achieve. We review them with the relevant department before quoting anything.

You receive a written plan: which hospital, which department, what is included, the estimated cost range, and how long you need to stay in China.

Only after you accept that plan do we ask for a deposit. No plan, no payment.

Scope of care

What we can help you reach

Indications and treatment plans are determined by the partner hospital after clinical assessment. We arrange access; we do not diagnose or promise outcomes.

01

Health checkup

Structured screening packages, imaging and specialist review, with an English report you can take home.

02

Specialist consultation

Second opinions and department-matched consultations across major tertiary hospitals.

03

Surgery

Elective procedures with pre-operative assessment, surgical scheduling and inpatient coordination.

04

Rehabilitation

Post-operative recovery, physiotherapy and supervised rehabilitation programmes.

Areas our partner hospitals commonly assess include oncology, cardiovascular care, orthopaedics, stem cell therapy and CAR-T programmes. Availability, eligibility and protocol are decided by the treating hospital, not by us.

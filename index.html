Tailored to your case

It starts at the airport, not at the hospital gate.

Most patients arriving in China face the same wall: registration queues, department hierarchies, reports nobody translates. We remove that wall. A coordinator meets you on arrival, stays with your case every day, and keeps your family briefed in plain English.

Clinical decisions belong to the hospital. Everything around them — the logistics, the language, the continuity — belongs to us.

The journey

Five steps, one person holding the thread

From the moment your flight lands to the follow-up call after you are home, the same coordinator stays with your case.

01

Arrival & pickup

Airport pickup, transfer to accommodation, SIM and payment setup, and a first briefing on what happens next.

02

Registration

Hospital registration, department matching and an in-person consultation with the specialist, interpreted line by line.

03

Examination

Imaging, laboratory work and specialist review, sequenced to avoid repeat tests and unnecessary waiting.

04

Treatment

Surgery or therapy, inpatient stay, nursing coordination and rehabilitation, with daily updates to your family.

05

Discharge & follow-up

English discharge summary, medication plan, return transfer, and scheduled follow-up once you are home.

How a case starts

Send us the diagnosis, the latest imaging and lab reports, and what you are hoping to achieve. We review them with the relevant department before quoting anything.

You receive a written plan: which hospital, which department, what is included, the estimated cost range, and how long you need to stay in China.

Only after you accept that plan do we ask for a deposit. No plan, no payment.
